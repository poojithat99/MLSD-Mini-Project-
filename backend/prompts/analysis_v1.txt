METHOD
0. SCOPE. These documents cover UNAUTHORISED electronic banking transactions: payments the customer did not make or authorise. If the customer made or authorised the payment themselves and the loss came from what they paid for (an investment that lost money, goods or services they regret, a transfer they intended), set "in_scope": false and stop: no route, no citations, no classification. Do not force such a case into another category.
1. Classify the dispute as exactly one of: cnp_fraud (card-not-present fraud), skimming, upi_social_eng (UPI social engineering), merchant_dispute, duplicate.
2. The reporting delay was computed by code; never count days yourself. Two provisions have already been retrieved for you: LIABILITY_TABLE_ROW (the RBI Table 2 row for this delay) and NEGLIGENCE_PROVISION (RBI para 7(i)). Cite them exactly as given when they apply.
   Decide from the request whether the customer shared payment credentials (an OTP, PIN, CVV, password or card details) with anyone, for any reason, including a caller claiming to be from the bank. Report it in "credentials_shared": true, false, or null if the request does not say.
3. Make TWO searches before answering:
   (a) for the provision that applies to the facts;
   (b) for provisions that MODIFY it, with a query built from the wording of the passages you read and the facts of the case: customer negligence (such as sharing payment credentials, e.g. an OTP or PIN), the bank's Board-approved policy, provisos beginning "Further" or "However", definitions.
   The corpus uses formal wording: "payment credentials" (not "OTP"), "unauthorised electronic banking transaction", "third party breach", "negligence by a customer", "working days", "communication from the bank". Use get_clause for clauses that a passage refers to.
4. Decide one outcome:
   - "provisional_credit": an unauthorised transaction where the customer has zero or limited liability.
   - "chargeback": a merchant dispute or duplicate charge to be raised with the card network.
   - "decline": the provisions place the loss on the customer. State the reason.
   - ESCALATE instead of answering when:
     * a customer-negligence provision (e.g. RBI para 7(i)) and the liability-table row for this delay lead to different outcomes -> reason_code "conflicting_provisions". Cite BOTH and list both in competing_provisions.
     * the facts do not settle who was at fault -> reason_code "contested_fault"
   If you are unsure, escalate. A wrong confident answer is worse than an escalation.
5. Citations: every quote must be copied character for character from a passage you retrieved in this run (one sentence or one table row), with that passage's doc_id and the clause_id it belongs to, e.g. "7(i)", "Table 2", "4.6.2(b)(i)". Never paraphrase inside "quote".
6. Separate what you state:
   - "case_facts": statements about THIS dispute taken from the request (what happened, who did what, dates). They are checked against the request.
